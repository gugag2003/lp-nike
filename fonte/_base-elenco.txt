STYLE (shared by every look in this series, repeat verbatim):
Ultra-realistic studio e-commerce fashion photograph of one adult man, a fictional person, full body, standing and facing the camera, looking straight into the lens, mouth closed, neutral relaxed expression. Vertical 2:3 portrait, 1024x1536.
Camera: placed at waist height, level, long portrait lens (about 85 mm on full frame), seen from far away, no looking-down perspective, no wide-angle distortion, both soles sitting flat on the same horizontal floor line, hip-width apart.
Framing with room to spare: whole body in frame, nothing cropped. Top of the head at 8% of the image height, soles of the shoes at 94% of the image height, the body takes 86% of the image height, centered horizontally, generous empty white on both sides.
Light: soft, high, frontal studio light, no hard shadows on the body, only a gentle volume in the fabric, true-to-life colors, natural skin texture, real fabric texture. Never plastic or CGI-looking.
Background: seamless pure white #FFFFFF, no horizon, no floor line, no gradient, no props. Only one very faint soft contact shadow directly under the shoes.
Clothes fit: oversized menswear streetwear, relaxed and draped, real weight in the fabric. The clothes hang off him with plenty of air inside; the volume comes from the fabric and never from his body.
Hands: anatomically correct, five fingers each, nothing held in them. Arms must not merge with the torso; keep clear silhouette edges.
No text, logos, prints or graphics on the sweater or the jeans, no embossed, tonal or woven pattern on any fabric. The only branding in the picture is the real branding on the sneakers. No belt, no glasses, no jewelry, no hat, no accessory in the hands, no T-shirt peeking out at the hem, no watermark.

FEET (every look):
Feet: a little apart, each foot turned outward about 25 to 30 degrees from the camera axis, so we see the toe, the laces and the outer side of each shoe, with the brand logo on the outer side clearly readable. Never duck-footed, never a pure side view. Both soles sit flat on the same horizontal floor line.

TROUSERS FALL (every look; this is the fall of the approved look 01):
The leg stays the same width from the knee to the bottom, straight and heavy, never tapering and never barrel or balloon shaped, with no gathering, no elastic and no ribbed band. A plain hem stacks in one or two soft folds as it lands on the shoe, covering the ankle collar and the top of the tongue. The toe, the front laces, the sole and the logo on the side of the shoe stay in plain view.
