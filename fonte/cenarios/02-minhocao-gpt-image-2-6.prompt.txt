Ultra-realistic photograph at night under the Minhocão elevated highway (Elevado Presidente João Goulart) in central São Paulo, Brazil. Massive raw concrete pillars and the underside of the elevated road deck form a long colonnade receding into the distance. The pillars and the surrounding old apartment building facades are covered in layered spray-paint graffiti and colorful throw-ups, and high on the building tops, rows of tall, thin, angular black rune-like letters typical of São Paulo rooftop tagging: abstract, illegible, no real words. Warm orange sodium streetlights mixed with a few cold white LED lights, faint reflections on a slightly damp asphalt and concrete floor, light urban haze.
Composition: straight-on, eye-level camera at 1.6 m height, 35mm lens, one-point perspective looking along the space under the highway, horizon at 45% of the frame height. The center and lower half of the frame are an open, empty, flat paved area where people could stand. No people, no cars, no animals, no readable words or letters anywhere, no brand names, no logos.
Shot on a full-frame camera, natural color, fine film grain, shallow depth of field with the far end slightly soft, editorial streetwear lookbook backdrop.
